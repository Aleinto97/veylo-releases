# Branding

An open **V**: two round strokes meeting at a point, the far one at half strength, like light falling across a screen. Bone `#f2efea` on a warm black squircle (`#26241f` to `#000`).

- `veylo-icon.svg` / `.png`: app icon. `veylo-symbol.svg`: transparent mark.
- Android launcher resources are in `apps/veylo-dx/android/res/` (adaptive + monochrome).
